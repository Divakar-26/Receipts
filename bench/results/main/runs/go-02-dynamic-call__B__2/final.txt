I renamed `ApplyDiscount` to `CalculateDiscount` everywhere, and nothing still depends on the old name. The build and the full test suite pass after the edit, and a final grep for `ApplyDiscount` finds nothing.

  - the `Pricer` method in `pricing/pricer.go`

- **Reflective lookup:** `jobs/jobs.go` finds the method by name with `MethodByName("Apply" + "Discount")`. Without that change, `jobs.Nightly()` would have panicked at runtime, because `Call` on an invalid method value panics.
